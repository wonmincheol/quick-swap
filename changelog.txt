Date: 2026-09-13
  Features:

Version: 0.2.8
Date: 2026-09-04
  Bugfixes:
    - Skip unavailable belt kinds during Shift-wheel cycling so partial belt groups still wrap.
  Features:
    - Support Shift-wheel and Control-wheel swaps in TAB/map view without requiring inventory items.
    - Keep the pre-swap quality in TAB/map and remote view while limiting items to force-unlocked recipes.
    - Read and replace the remote-view cursor ghost so wheel swaps work without a physical cursor stack.
    - Enable all four custom inputs while spectating so remote/map controllers receive the wheel events.
    - Preserve legendary remote cursor quality without rejecting all swap candidates through a redundant quality-unlock check.
    - Cycle between normal and underground pipes with Shift + mouse wheel.
    - Step through electric poles in ascending or descending order with Shift + mouse wheel, without wrapping at either end.
    - Skip unavailable intermediate electric poles when moving in either direction.
    - Keep belt, pipe, and electric-pole swaps isolated to their own groups.
    - Use the highest available target quality when swapping from character inventory.
  Changes:
    - Preserve vanilla wheel actions when no swap occurs, including blueprint-book cycling and normal zoom.
    - Temporarily lock the current zoom during a successful swap instead of restoring the camera after it moves.
